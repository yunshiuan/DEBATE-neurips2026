Your task is to analyze a post-conversation opinion generated by either a human or a role-playing LLM. After having a conversation about the topic "{TOPIC}", the person was asked to write whether they agree or disagree with the topic and explain why in 2-3 sentences. You have to determine if the post-conversation opinion is "valid and relevant".

A valid post-conversation opinion should:

1. Be relevant to the topic of interest. They may use pronouns like “it” or “this” to refer to the topic and that is fine as long as you can reasonably infer.
2. Be properly formatted (not contain instructions, JSON objects, or metadata)
