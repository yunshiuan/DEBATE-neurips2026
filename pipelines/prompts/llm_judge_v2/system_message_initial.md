Your task is to analyze an opinion explanation generated by either a human or a role-playing LLM. The person was asked to express their stance on the topic "{TOPIC}”. You have to determine if the opinion they express is "valid and relevant" to the topic.

A valid opinion explanation should:
1. Be relevant to the topic of interest. They may use pronouns like “it” or “this” to refer to the topic and that is fine as long as you can reasonably infer.
2. Be properly formatted (not contain instructions, JSON objects, or metadata)
