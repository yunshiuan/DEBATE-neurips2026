# Post Opinion Validity Judge

Your task is to analyze a post-conversation opinion generated by either a human or a role-playing LLM. After discussing the topic "{TOPIC}", the person was asked to state whether they agree or disagree with the topic and explain why. You must decide whether the post-conversation opinion is a valid answer to that prompt.

Mark the opinion as VALID only if all of the following are true:

1. It contains an explicit stance, or a clearly inferable stance, toward the topic. The answer must communicate agreement, disagreement, or a clearly directional lean. Generic chat, placeholders, greetings, or meta comments do not count as a stance.
2. It contains a topic-grounded explanation, justification, or final-topic summary. A brief explanation is enough if it makes the final stance clear. For post opinions, a short but clearly directional final answer can still be VALID when it plainly states the speaker's end position on the topic, even if the justification is concise.
3. It is relevant to the topic. Pronouns like "it" or "this" are acceptable if the topic reference is clearly recoverable from the post-opinion prompt and the rest of the answer still comments on that claim. In a post-opinion response, default to interpreting standalone references like "this", "it", or "that's" as the discussion claim unless the answer clearly points somewhere else. Short final answers such as "This is just not true" or "I don't think that's right" can be VALID when they are plainly referring to the discussion topic and express a clear end stance.
4. It is coherent natural language, not gibberish, nonsense strings, malformed placeholders, repeated metadata markers, or unrelated content.
5. Minor wrapper artifacts such as "My Response:" or "My Opinion:" are acceptable if the underlying content is still a clear, substantive, topic-grounded answer. Mark the opinion INVALID only if the artifact is the whole answer, dominates the answer, or the remaining content is not a valid response.

Mark the opinion as INVALID if any of the following apply:

- It does not state a stance.
- It gives no topic-grounded reason or final-topic summary at all, and the stance does not stand on its own as a clear final position on the topic.
- It is mostly greeting, small talk, filler, or a chat continuation.
- It is obviously off-topic.
- It is nonsense, truncated, repetitive, or malformed.
- It is just a template fragment such as "My Response", "My Opinion", "No comments", or similar placeholders, without substantive topic-grounded content.

Be strict about missing stance, off-topic content, or malformed text. Do not reject an otherwise valid answer solely because it is brief, especially when the brief answer still gives a clear final stance about the topic, or because it includes a light wrapper artifact.
Do not require the answer to restate the topic noun phrase explicitly when the stance is otherwise plainly expressed in direct response to the post-opinion prompt.
